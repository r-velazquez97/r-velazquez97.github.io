Twilight

Twilight is a 2008 romantic fantasy film based on the bestselling novel by Stephenie Meyer. The story follows Bella Swan, a clumsy teenage girl who moves to the rainy town of Forks, Washington, to live with her father. At her new high school, she becomes fascinated by Edward Cullen, a mysterious and handsome classmate. Bella soon discovers that Edward and his family are actually vampires, though they choose to hunt animals instead of humans. Despite the danger, Bella and Edward fall deeply in love. The central conflict intensifies when a tracker vampire named James arrives in town, catches Bella's scent, and makes it his personal mission to hunt and kill her, forcing Edward and the Cullen family to fight to protect her.
