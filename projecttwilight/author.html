About the author

• She published the first novel, Twilight, in 2005, which quickly exploded into a global phenomenon and laid the foundation for the entire movie franchise and companion universe you are building on your website.

• Stephenie Meyer wrote the original book, so she visited the movie set frequently to provide guidance on the characters.She spent hours talking with director Catherine Hardwicke to explain how the vampires should look, move, and act. Catherine shared the script drafts and casting ideas with Stephenie to get her approval before filming.Stephenie even helped choose specific songs for the iconic movie soundtrack.Catherine focused on the visual directing, while Stephenie made sure the movie stayed completely true to her book.
